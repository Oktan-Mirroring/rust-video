## Format

Chroma (Table 6-1, 6.2): 0 mono, 1 4:2:0, 2 4:2:2, 3 4:4:4; absent means
4:2:0 (7.4.2.1.1). Bit depth = 8 + bit_depth_minus8, range 8-14 bit;
High 8-bit, High 10 / 4:2:2 at most 10-bit, 4:4:4 at most 14-bit (A.2.4-7).
Slice types (Table 7-6, 7.4.3): 0/5 P, 1/6 B, 2/7 I, 3/8 SP, 4/9 SI; IDR
holds I/SI only. FMO map types 0-6 over at most 8 groups (7.3.2.2, 8.2.1),
Baseline/Extended only. ASO (3.6) is banned in Main/High (A.2.2, A.2.4).

## NAL (Table 7-1, 7.4.1)

Types 0-31: 0 unspecified, 1 slice, 2/3/4 partitions A/B/C, 5 IDR, 6 SEI,
7 SPS, 8 PPS, 9 AUD, 10 end of sequence, 11 end of stream, 12 filler,
13 SPS extension, 14 prefix, 15 subset SPS, 16-18 reserved, 19 auxiliary,
20 extension, 21-23 reserved, 24-31 unspecified. forbidden_zero_bit is
always 0. Emulation prevention inserts 0x03 to ban 000000/000001/000002
byte triples; the last byte is never 0. nal_ref_idc is 0 for types
6/9-12 and nonzero for 5/7/8. An IDR holds no type 1-4 NALs. Partitions
2-4 are banned in Baseline/Main/High (A.2). Annex A drops 14/15/20.
Annex B uses 0x000001 start codes with zero_byte and cabac_zero_word.

## HRD (Annex C, E)

CPB plus DPB model; NAL rates are 1.2x VCL. SchedSelIdx runs at most
cpb_cnt_minus1 (at most 31). Buffering period plus picture timing SEI
messages are required. MinCR caps access-unit size (A.3.1c,d; High
A.3.3i,j; none for High 10 and up).
